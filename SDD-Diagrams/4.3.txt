' Define Foreign Key (FK) Dependencies
' JobDataModule depends on UserDataModule

' MessagingDataModule depends on UserDataModule and JobDataModule
MDM --> UDM : FK: SenderID, ReceiverID (Messages linked to Users)
MDM --> JDM : FK: JobID/ProposalID (Messages contextualized by Jobs)
